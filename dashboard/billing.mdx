Takumo uses a credit-based system. Each plan includes a credit allowance. Operations consume credits.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Included Credits |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Free | $0 | 1,000/mo |
| Pro | $19/mo | 5,000/mo |
| Enterprise | $29/mo per seat | 5,000/seat/mo |
| Custom | Custom | Unlimited |

## Credit Costs per Operation

Every operation that Takumo performs consumes a specific number of credits:

| Operation | Credits |
|-----------|---------|
| Shield scan | 1 |
| Sentinel review | 2 |
| Fix suggestion | 3 |
| Code review | 5 |
| Policy evaluation | 1 |
| Real-time scan | 1 |

A Shield scan is the cheapest operation at 1 credit. A full code review is the most expensive at 5 credits. Most day-to-day usage is Shield scans and policy evaluations, both at 1 credit each.

## Overage

What happens when you exceed your monthly credit allowance depends on your plan:

| Plan | Overage Behavior |
|------|-----------------|
| Free | Hard limit. Operations are blocked until your credits reset. Free credits reset every month. |
| Pro | $0.02 per credit over allowance |
| Enterprise | $0.02 per credit over allowance |
| Custom | Included. No overage charges. The Custom plan is the only one with no per credit overage price. |

<Note>Enterprise plan credits scale with your team. 5 seats = 25,000 credits/month.</Note>

### How overage is billed

Takumo records the overage it detects as you use credits, and reports it to your billing account in small amounts during the billing period, about every five minutes. It is not saved up for the end of the period, because the invoice for a period is cut before a report made after it would count. Each report is sent once: a retry never charges you twice. Reported overage appears as a usage line on your next invoice.

The Billing page, Settings > Billing and the Usage page show where your overage stands for the current period:

| Line | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Reported to billing** | Overage already sent to your billing account. |
| **Waiting to be reported** | Overage Takumo has recorded and will send on its next run. |
| **Not billable yet** | Overage recorded while your subscription did not yet include overage billing. It is billed once the subscription does, within that period's spending limit. |
| **Could not be reported** | Overage Polar refused. Contact support. |
| **Carried to the next billing period** | Overage over this period's spending limit. It is reported in the next period, within that period's limit. |

Things to know:

- **Spending limit.** The limit you set on the Billing page applies to the overage you have already accrued plus the action that would add to it. An action that would take your overage past the limit is refused, so detected overage never passes the limit within a period. The limit is checked again when overage is reported, against the billing period the report falls in. Overage held back for a missing meter, or left over from the end of a period, is reported only as far as that period's limit leaves room. What does not fit waits for the next period and is never dropped.
- **Cancelling.** If you cancel, overage keeps being reported until your paid period ends and goes on your final invoice. Overage recorded after the subscription has ended is not billed.
- **Annual plans.** Annual subscriptions are invoiced for overage at renewal, not monthly. The Billing page says so.
- **Timing at the period boundary.** Overage from the last few minutes before your billing period is cut can appear on the next invoice, so one invoice can run a few cents past your spending limit.
- **Refunds.** If credits are refunded after the overage was reported, Takumo reports a matching reduction, and the next invoice nets it.

## Managing Your Subscription

### Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Billing">
    Navigate to **Settings > Billing** in the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a plan">
    Choose the plan you want to upgrade to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The upgrade takes effect immediately. You get access to the new plan's features and credit allowance right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Downgrading

When you cancel, the plan changes at the end of the current billing period and you keep your current plan's features until then. A switch to a lower paid plan takes effect when the billing provider confirms it, and the Billing page always shows the plan you are on.

If you're downgrading from a plan that includes features you're actively using (e.g., fleet management on Enterprise, SSO on Custom), those features will become unavailable when the downgrade takes effect.

Takumo deletes nothing when a plan drops. If you use more than the new plan includes, such as more API keys or members, what you have keeps working and new ones are refused until you are under the limit. Each affected page shows an **Over your plan limit** banner, and the email about the plan change lists each limit you are over. Audit history becomes a shorter view window, and older entries return if you upgrade. See [Limits](/dashboard/limits#after-a-downgrade).

When a paid plan ends, the organization starts a new Free period with a fresh credit balance.

## Monitoring Usage

The Billing page shows:

- **Current plan** and renewal date
- **Credits used** this billing period vs. your allowance
- **Credit burn rate**: how quickly you're consuming credits based on recent usage
- **Overage charges**: any charges beyond your included allowance
- **Invoices**: your orders with date, amount and status, and a download for each invoice. The billing portal link stays next to them

A Custom plan without a subscription shows **Billed by agreement**. Your account team sends your invoices, so there is no payment method to add.
