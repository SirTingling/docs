## Gateway rate limits

| Gateway requests a minute | Free | Pro | Enterprise | Custom |
|---------------------------|------|-----------|-----|------------|
| Per organization | 100 | 1,000 | 5,000 | 20,000 |

Takumo does not enforce per minute or per month limits by operation. Credits are the one metered cap. See [Rate Limits](/api/rate-limits) for how the gateway applies these numbers.

## Overage

Pro and Enterprise plans charge **$0.02 per credit** over the included amount. A request that is already running is never cut off. If you set a spending limit on the Billing page, new actions in the dashboard are refused with `402` and `spending_limit_reached` once the overage would pass it. Usage is billed at the end of the billing cycle.

Custom plans include unlimited credits. No overage charges.

Free plans are hard-capped. When you hit the limit, actions that Takumo runs for you are refused before they start, and the API returns `402` with `limit_exceeded:creditsPerPeriod`. Daemon transforms are refused at the credit cap too: the daemon checks your credits before it starts the work and shows the plan message. If Takumo Cloud cannot be reached, the transform runs and the charge is recorded afterwards. Usage that lands past the cap this way shows on the Usage page as over the cap, not charged. Free credits reset every month.
